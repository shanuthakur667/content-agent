# Risk rules

These exist so a single wrong line cannot get the channel a strike, a takedown, or a legal letter. The fact-checker fails a draft that breaks them.

## Doping, bans, disqualifications
- Only state a ban/DQ/positive test if an official body (World Athletics, AIU, WADA, Ironman, HYROX, national federation) or a major outlet citing one has reported it. Cite that `url:`.
- Use "reported", "provisionally suspended", "according to [source]". Never "cheater", never "obviously doped", never speculate about an athlete who has not been officially sanctioned.
- Ongoing cases: say "alleged" and "the athlete denies / has not commented" if that is the case.

## Health, nutrition, injury, supplements
- Report what a study found, in whom, and its size ("a 2026 study of 24 trained runners found..."). Never generalise a small study into advice.
- No dosages presented as instructions. "The protocol in the study used X" is fine; "take X" is not.
- Injuries: describe, never diagnose. One light "if this is you, see a physio" per script, not a lecture.
- Never promise outcomes ("this will make you faster").

## Records and results
- Times, distances, splits, placings: cite the official result page or a major outlet quoting it. Prefer official (worldathletics.org, ironman.com, hyrox.com, race sites, Strava/official athlete posts).
- Distinguish world record vs world best vs course record vs national record. If unsure, say the more conservative one and note it.
- Unratified performances must be labelled "pending ratification".

## Naming real people
- Public athletes: name freely for their public performances and public statements.
- Private individuals (forum posters, commenters): never name, never quote identifiably. Summarise the sentiment instead.
- Never attribute motives or character judgements to real people. Describe actions and quotes.

## Footage and images
- Every B-roll line carries a rights tag: `(creator-shot)`, `(licensed)`, or `(fair-use commentary)`.
- Fair-use commentary means a short clip you are actively talking over and analysing, not background wallpaper. Broadcast race footage is the riskiest category — default to stills, your own footage, or licensed clips.

## The creator himself
- Anything about Yugansh's own training, times, body, or experiences must be a `[YUGANSH: ...]` placeholder for him to fill. Never invented.
