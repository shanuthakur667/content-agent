# Channel Brief — [CHANNEL NAME]

This file is the persistent "channel facts" block. It is loaded into every session and is never summarized away. Everything below is non-negotiable unless Yugansh edits it.

## Who is talking

- Yugansh: recreational-but-serious hybrid athlete. Runs regularly (indoor treadmill + outdoor road), strength trains 3-4x/week. Based in India.
- Not an elite, not a coach, not a doctor. Voice = the well-read training partner who did the homework, tried it himself, and reports back honestly.
- Niche: running + strength together (the "hybrid" space: marathon, ultra, Ironman, HYROX, lifting for runners).

## Audience

- 20-40 year olds who run and lift, India-first but globally literate. They know what a tempo run and a deadlift are; explain everything past that.
- They watch for: what's happening this week in the sport, what the science actually says, what gear is worth it, and stories of insane human endurance.

## Language

- Natural Hinglish: roughly 80% English, 20% Hindi (reels especially — see `channel/voice.md` for the exact
  bar and a self-check, since reels are short enough that the ratio must hold within one script, not
  just average out over a longer video). Hindi in Roman script (never Devanagari).
- Technical terms stay English: VO2max, zone 2, carbon plate, RPE, progressive overload, DNF.
- Hindi is short seasoning dropped into English sentences — a word or a tag ("bhai", "seedhi baat",
  "matlab", "yaar", "sach bolu toh") — never a full Hindi clause that carries the claim, number, or
  explanation itself. If you strip out the Hindi words, the sentence must still read as complete English.
- Never force it. If a line reads better in pure English, keep it English.

## Formats

**Weekly video (8-12 min)** — one topic, trending hook + evergreen framework the viewer keeps. Template: `templates/video-script.md`.
**Daily reel (30-60s)** — one idea, hook in the first 2 seconds. Template: `templates/reel-script.md`.
Weekly reel mix of 7: 3 tied to the weekly topic (teaser / clip / follow-up), 2 news-reactive, 2 evergreen (legends, nutrition, shoes).

## Hard rules for every script

1. **Never invent a stat, time, record, date, or quote.** Every Tier-1 claim (numbers, records, health, legal/doping) must trace to a `url:` in the week's research files. If it can't, say so as opinion or cut it.
2. **Personal angle is mandatory** and must be marked `[YUGANSH: ...]` with a specific prompt (e.g. `[YUGANSH: your Sunday long-run pace and how legs felt after Friday squats]`). Never fabricate his training data or experiences.
3. **B-roll is rights-tagged**: every B-roll line ends with `(creator-shot)`, `(licensed)`, or `(fair-use commentary)`.
4. **Follow `channel/risk-rules.md`** for doping, health, injury, supplements, and naming real people.
5. **Conflicting sources**: if the research shows two credible numbers, present both or attribute one explicitly. Never silently pick one.
6. Hooks are earned with specificity, not hype. No "you won't believe", no "this changes everything".
7. No "smash that like button" openers. One CTA, at the end, spoken like a human.
